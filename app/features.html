Team Pool Tracker

Fair games. Less chalkboard. More pool.

Team Pool Tracker takes the scribbling, arguing, and guessing out of running a team pool match. Open it on your phone, tap who showed up, and it builds a fair schedule for the day. It is free, it needs no account, and it works with no signal in the pool room.

Why managers like it

  • Everyone gets a fair turn. It builds doubles pairings from whoever shows up, from 4 players to 15 or more, and balances who plays and who partners with whom.
  • Late arrivals are no problem. Add a player mid-match, even someone not on the roster, and the app slots them into the games still waiting without wrecking anyone else's plan.
  • You stay in charge. Swap any player, lock two partners together, unlock them later, or drop games when time runs short. The app suggests; the manager decides.
  • It keeps up with the clock. A pace line says On pace, Ahead, or Running behind, based on how long games really take.
  • Scoring is two taps. Tap Win or Loss for the table and the next game starts by itself. Made a mistake? Undo.
  • Paper when you need it. Print a blank score sheet as a backup, then print or save a clean record of the match.

Built for a senior league

  • Big buttons, plain words. One question per screen. No jargon and no pop-up boxes.
  • Nothing to sign up for. No account, no password, no app store. Open the link and add it to your home screen.
  • Works without internet. After the first visit it runs offline.
  • Your data stays yours. Everything is stored on your own phone. Nothing is tracked or sent anywhere.
  • Easy updates. One tap on Update now keeps the latest version, and your players and matches stay put.

Know where the team stands

  • Team stats, manager only. Wins and losses, win percentage, best partner combinations, and home versus away results, behind a private PIN.
  • Practice matches. Try a match out, or teach a new player the screens, without touching the real record.
  • Roster that stays tidy. Mark occasional players as Subs, fix a misspelled name, remove a player, or make someone inactive and bring them back later without losing history.

Share it in seconds

  • Send the roster to another phone by text message.
  • Send the match results to the manager, or text the score to the team.
  • Hand the manager role to someone else when the time comes.
